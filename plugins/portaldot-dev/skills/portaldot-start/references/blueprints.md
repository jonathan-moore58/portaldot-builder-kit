# Blueprints: one plan per kind of project

By the Bridge Zero team, an independent project and not an official Portaldot one. One section per kind of project, read together with the start guide (`portaldot-start`), for example `read_guide {"topic": "blueprints/token"}`. Each has the same seven parts in the same order: Choose it when, The chain has, Decide (the question to ask, its default, and when to go the other way), Traps, Plan, Costs, Not known.

Every fact names the section of the knowledge pack that holds it, in brackets: [errors/E41] is `read_guide {"topic": "errors/E41"}`. Amounts are testnet values, measured on the dates those sections give; before a send, take the real number from the dry run the plan names. Every step that sends follows the send loop of the start guide [portaldot-start/6], and the developer signs it. "The contract route" is the one in [portaldot-start/4].

## Token

- **Choose it when:** points, a reward or game currency, shares, a test stablecoin, a token that MetaMask users or contracts must use.
- **The chain has:** the Assets module: `Assets.create(id, admin, minBalance)` 0x2700, `Assets.mint` 0x2706, `Assets.transfer` 0x2708 [native-modules/everyday-jobs-calls], and `Assets.setMetadata` [native-call-errors/assets]. Every asset answers ERC-20 calls (`transfer`, `approve`, `transferFrom`, `permit`) at its id as 4 big-endian bytes, 12 zero bytes, then `0001` and `0000`: asset 2 is `0x0000000200000000000000000000000000010000` [contracts/tokens-assets-module-seen]. The native pools can trade it [blueprints/swap-dex].
- **Decide:** "Must every transfer run rules of your own?" Default: no, an Assets token: 1 tPOT of deposit, and 0.1 + 0.06 per byte for its metadata [native-modules/everyday-jobs-calls], where a Solidity ERC-20 of 1863 bytes held 158.424 tPOT for its code and 23.049 for its 6 slots [recipes-contracts/2d]. Yes: a Solidity ERC-20 on the contract route, which the Assets module and the native pools do not see [contracts/separate-token-worlds].
- **Traps:**
  - A token that is not sufficient cannot reach an account with no tPOT (`Token(CannotCreate)`); a new holder needs at least `minBalance` (`Token(BelowMinimum)`); a `minBalance` of 0 is refused; only the issuer mints [native-call-errors/assets].
  - At the ERC-20 address: a calling contract gets no reason for a failure [errors/E40]; each approval or permit holds 0.1 tPOT from the owner, outside gas [errors/E41]; a transfer that would leave less than `minBalance` moves the whole balance, while the log shows the amount asked [errors/E42]; "unlimited" is stored as 2^128 - 1 and goes down [errors/E43]; tPOT sent with a token call stays there [errors/E44].
  - Without metadata, `name()`, `symbol()` and `decimals()` revert [errors/E26]; read `decimals()`, never assume 18 [contracts/tokens-assets-module-seen].
  - `admin` and `beneficiary` are native accounts: a bare 0x string fails with `CannotLookup` [errors/E50]; a MetaMask holder is its fallback 5... form [errors/E4].
- **Plan:**
  1. A free id: `read_storage {"item": "Assets.asset", "keys": [<id>]}` must be empty; a taken id answers `Assets.InUse` [native-call-errors/assets].
  2. Create, set the metadata and mint in one `Utility.batchAll` from the owner's native account; `describe_item` gives each call's arguments. Dry run: `encode_call` of the batch with `"sender"`; it names an inner error [native-call-errors/utility], and `Balances.InsufficientBalance` means the deposits cannot be paid [native-call-errors/replies-whose-name-misleads]. Check: `read_storage` of `Assets.asset(<id>)` (owner, supply, `minBalance`, `isSufficient`, status `Live`) and `Assets.metadata(<id>)`. From a 0x key, send the three calls one by one through the pallets address [recipes-contracts/4]: a batch sent that way is Reported only [open-questions/not-known].
  3. The ERC-20 side: `convert_address` of the token address names the asset; `simulate_contract_call` there of `decimals()` (`0x313ce567`) [recipes-contracts/3] and `balanceOf`.
  4. First move: `Assets.transfer` (dry run: `encode_call` with `"sender"`), or `transfer` at the token address from a 0x key (dry run: `estimate_eth_transaction`, verdict TAKEN) [recipes-contracts/2g]. Check: `get_transaction`, then `read_storage {"item": "Assets.account", "keys": [<id>, "<5...>"]}` or `balanceOf` of both sides.
- **Costs:** asset 1 tPOT and metadata 0.1 + 0.06 per byte, reserved; the metadata base is 0.5 POT on the mainnet [native-modules/everyday-jobs-calls, native-modules/69-modules]. Fees: `Assets.transfer` 0.0146 tPOT; at the token address `transfer` or `approve` 0.0187, `transferFrom` 0.0219, `permit` 0.0348 [amounts-and-fees/native-fees]. The fees of `create`, `setMetadata` and `mint`: `encode_call` with `"sender"`.
- **Not known:** sent `Assets.create`, `setMetadata` and `mint` (their rows in these notes are dry runs [native-call-errors/intro]; 52 assets existed on the testnet on Nov 14, 2026 [native-modules/testnet-holds]); writes through PoolAssets addresses, and a sufficient asset [evm-checks/3]; whether an approval's 0.1 tPOT comes back when `transferFrom` uses it all [errors/E41]; when the asset deposit comes back.

## NFT collection

- **Choose it when:** collectibles, tickets, badges, certificates, game items, a mint page.
- **The chain has:** the Nfts module: `Nfts.create(admin, config)` 0x2f00 and `Nfts.mint(collection, item, mintTo, witnessData)` 0x2f03 [native-modules/everyday-jobs-calls], with `Nfts.transfer` and `Nfts.burn` [native-call-errors/nfts]; 28 collections on the testnet on Nov 14, 2026 [native-modules/testnet-holds]. Uniques held no classes there: use Nfts [native-call-errors/uniques].
- **Decide:** "Must MetaMask show the items, or a contract own or check them?" Default: no, Nfts: 1 tPOT per collection and 0.1 tPOT per item [native-modules/everyday-jobs-calls]; the page reads ownership from the node, and a MetaMask user's items sit on its fallback 5... form [errors/E4]. Yes: a Solidity ERC-721 on the contract route, since no ERC-721 address is known for Nfts [contracts/separate-token-worlds]; it holds 0.078 x L + 13.11 tPOT for its code [amounts-and-fees/deploying-contract] and 3.8415 tPOT per new slot a mint writes, paid by the mint's sender [amounts-and-fees/contract-storage-slot]: dry-run one mint before you price the collection.
- **Traps:**
  - `Nfts.mint` into a collection that does not exist answers `Nfts.NoConfig`; `Nfts.transfer` then answers `UnknownCollection`, whose text speaks of an item [native-call-errors/replies-whose-name-misleads].
  - With mint type Issuer only the issuer mints (`Nfts.NoPermission`); a used item id answers `Nfts.AlreadyExists`; a creator that cannot pay the deposit gets `Balances.InsufficientBalance` [native-call-errors/nfts].
  - `mintTo` is a native account: a bare 0x string fails with `CannotLookup` [errors/E50].
  - The "Account" attribute namespace needs `Nfts.approveItemAttributes` by the item's owner first (upstream code, not tested on V3) [errors/E25].
  - `block.prevrandao` is the same constant in every block: never use it for random traits [contracts/evm].
  - On the mainnet an item holds 0.05 POT, and a metadata or attribute base 0.5 POT [native-modules/69-modules].
- **Plan (Nfts):**
  1. Shapes and prices now: `describe_item {"name": "Nfts.create"}` (the config and its mint settings) and `describe_item {"name": "Nfts"}` (the metadata calls, the constants); `read_storage {"item": "Nfts.collectionDeposit"}` reads a deposit live.
  2. Create the collection from the issuer's native account. Dry run: `encode_call` of `Nfts.create` with `"sender"`. Check: `get_transaction` lists the new collection's id among the events; then `read_storage {"item": "Nfts.collection", "keys": [<id>]}`.
  3. Mint one item to the developer's account, then one to a MetaMask test user's fallback 5... form from `convert_address`. Dry run: `encode_call` of `Nfts.mint` with the issuer as `"sender"`. Check: `read_storage` of `Nfts.item(<collection>, <item>)` names the owner. Before the collection exists, a mint alone answers `Nfts.NoConfig` [native-call-errors/nfts]: price both with `encode_call` of a `Utility.batchAll` of `Nfts.create` and `Nfts.mint`, the mint into the id that `read_storage {"item": "Nfts.nextCollectionId"}` gives [native-modules/everyday-jobs-calls].
  4. Metadata for the collection and the items (the calls `describe_item` names), each dry-run; many mints in one `Utility.batchAll`, dry-run as a whole [native-call-errors/utility].
  5. The page: [blueprints/dapp-front-end]. A buyer on MetaMask pays the issuer's 0x form only after `convert_address` shows it linked to the issuer [addresses/form-use]; a bot of the issuer that follows its account's events by block then mints to the buyer's fallback form [blueprints/bot-indexer-keeper].
- **Costs:** a collection 1 tPOT; an item 0.1 tPOT; metadata and attribute bases 0.1 tPOT each [native-modules/everyday-jobs-calls, native-modules/69-modules]. The per-byte metadata deposit and the fees of `create` and `mint`: `describe_item` and `encode_call` with `"sender"`. An ERC-721: `simulate_contract_deploy`, then `simulate_contract_call` of one mint.
- **Not known:** the mint types Public and HolderOf, and item attributes [native-call-errors/not-measured]; sent Nfts calls (their rows in these notes are dry runs [native-call-errors/intro]); any way for contract code or MetaMask to see Nfts items [contracts/tokens-assets-module-seen]; the deposit of one ERC-721 mint.

## Swap or DEX

- **Choose it when:** swapping one token for another, a price from a pool, liquidity, a trading front end, test pUSD for users.
- **The chain has:** the AssetConversion module: `AssetConversion.swapExactTokensForTokens(path, amountIn, amountOutMin, sendTo, keepAlive)` 0x4103 [native-modules/everyday-jobs-calls], `createPool`, `addLiquidity` and `removeLiquidity` [native-call-errors/assetconversion]; quotes from the runtime call `assetConversionApi.quotePriceExactTokensForTokens`, in a script [recipes-contracts/2g]; 21 pools on the testnet on Nov 14, 2026 [native-modules/testnet-holds], one of them tPOT and pUSD (asset 7777) [native-call-errors/assetconversion]. LP tokens are PoolAssets, at addresses with `0002` where Assets have `0001` [contracts/tokens-assets-module-seen], with no metadata [evm-checks/3].
- **Decide:** "The existing pools, or a pricing rule of your own?" Default: the pools, with your page or bot in front. A rule of your own (an order book, a curve, a sealed bid) is a contract on the contract route, with its deposits [amounts-and-fees/deploying-contract], holding Assets tokens at their ERC-20 addresses [contracts/tokens-assets-module-seen].
- **Traps:**
  - A pair with no pool answers `AssetConversion.PoolEmpty`, whose text says the pool exists: read `AssetConversion.pools` first [native-call-errors/replies-whose-name-misleads].
  - Quote first: a minimum above what the pool pays fails with `ProvidedMinimumNotSufficientForSwap`. The pools are small: tPOT and pUSD held 177.5 tPOT and 126,827,790 units on Nov 15, 2026 [native-call-errors/assetconversion].
  - The receiver of a token that is not sufficient, such as pUSD, must already hold tPOT [native-call-errors/assets]; for a MetaMask user, `sendTo` is its fallback 5... form [recipes-contracts/2g].
  - From an empty account a swap answers `Token(NotExpendable)` or `Token(FundsUnavailable)`; `removeLiquidity` errors come from the amount, not from your holding [native-call-errors/replies-whose-name-misleads].
  - Amounts: tPOT has 14 decimals natively [errors/E3], pUSD 6 [contracts/tokens-assets-module-seen].
- **Plan:**
  1. The pool: `read_storage {"item": "AssetConversion.pools", "keys": [["Native", {"WithId": 7777}]]}` shows its `lpToken`; empty means no pool.
  2. Quote, then swap 1 tPOT as in [recipes-contracts/2g] step 1: `amountOutMin` 90 percent of the quote, `keepAlive` true. Dry run: `encode_call` with `"sender"`. Check: `get_transaction`, then `read_storage {"item": "Assets.account", "keys": [7777, "<5...>"]}`.
  3. Liquidity: `addLiquidity` with amounts above 0, the LP balance in `PoolAssets.account(<lpToken>, <account>)`; a new pair: `createPool`, then `addLiquidity` [native-call-errors/assetconversion]. Dry-run each with `encode_call`.
  4. From MetaMask: the same call bytes through the pallets address, value 0 [recipes-contracts/4]: `encode_call` with the 0x sender, then `estimate_eth_transaction`.
- **Costs:** a swap and a transfer in one `Utility.batchAll` paid 0.0226 tPOT [amounts-and-fees/native-fees]; 1 tPOT bought 708367 units of pUSD on Nov 15, 2026, exactly the quote [native-call-errors/assetconversion]. The deposit of `createPool` and a pool's fee rate: dry-run it.
- **Not known:** pool calls sent from a 0x account (dry runs only) [native-call-errors/measured]; writes through PoolAssets addresses [evm-checks/3]; fees paid in another asset [amounts-and-fees/paying-fees-another-asset]; the mainnet had no pools on Nov 14, 2026 [networks/mainnet]; a way for contract code to use the pools.

## Payments and payouts

- **Choose it when:** tips, invoices, a checkout, payroll, rewards, airdrops, pay per call, vesting: tPOT or a token moves from one account to many, or from many to one.
- **The chain has:** `Balances.transferKeepAlive` 0x0603, `Assets.transfer` 0x2708, and `Utility.batchAll` 0x0102, all or nothing, up to 10922 calls [native-modules/everyday-jobs-calls]; `System.remarkWithEvent`, whose event names the sender and the hash of the text, for an invoice or run id in the same batch [native-modules/call-encoding]; `Vesting.vestedTransfer` for a release over time [native-call-errors/vesting].
- **Decide:** "Pay from a script of yours, or let payees claim from a contract?" Default: a native script with `Utility.batchAll`. A contract when payees claim themselves or a rule must hold on the chain; contract code that pays a new address makes the transaction's sender pay that account's 0.1 tPOT, about 100 tPOT for 1000 new payees [errors/E54]. The Scheduler refuses a signed account [native-call-errors/scheduler], so a payout every day is a bot of yours [blueprints/bot-indexer-keeper].
- **Traps:**
  - A payment to the wrong address form is lost with no warning: 8 testnet accounts at likely wrong conversions held 8630.3 tPOT on Nov 15, 2026 [addresses/wrong-conversion-rules]. Run `convert_address` on every payee; never `addressToEvm`, `evmToAddress` or an `ethereum` keyring [errors/E4]; give @polkadot/api `{ Id: ... }`, never a bare 0x string [errors/E50].
  - 14 against 18 decimals: `parseEther` for a native amount pays 10000 times too much [errors/E3].
  - A native payment that creates an account must be at least 0.1 tPOT (`Token(BelowMinimum)`) [errors/E17], and in a `batchAll` one failing payment fails them all [native-modules/everyday-jobs-calls]. The payee of a token that is not sufficient needs tPOT first [native-call-errors/assets].
  - The sender can send at most free - max(frozen - reserved, 0.1 tPOT), less the fee; one base unit more answers `Token(Frozen)` [errors/E47].
  - A retry after an unclear send can pay twice: settle it by nonce first [errors/E12, recipes/9]. A payment of 0, or to the sender itself, is accepted: refuse it in code [native-call-errors/balances].
- **Plan:**
  1. Every payee: the form to pay and whether it exists, in the script for a list (recipe 6 for the form, recipe 7 for the account [recipes/6, recipes/7]; a MetaMask user is paid at its fallback 5... form); `convert_address` and `get_account` on a few of them while you build it; for a token, also `Assets.account(<id>, <payee>)`, which `read_storage` shows.
  2. Amounts: `convert_amount` for tPOT; a token's `decimals()` or `Assets.metadata`.
  3. Prove the setup with one small transfer [recipes/1]. Dry run: `encode_call` with `"sender"`. Check: `get_transaction` (finalized, `Balances.Transfer`, the fee) and `get_account` of the payee.
  4. The batch: one `Utility.batchAll` of the transfers and a `System.remarkWithEvent` with the invoice or run id. Dry run: `encode_call` of the exact batch with `"sender"`; it names an inner error [native-call-errors/utility]. Check: `get_transaction` lists every transfer; record the hash and block.
  5. Payments in: a payer on MetaMask pays your 0x form only after `convert_address` shows it linked to your native account [addresses/form-use]; watch your account's events by block [blueprints/bot-indexer-keeper] and match the remark.
  6. Vesting: `Vesting.vestedTransfer` (`describe_item` gives the schedule); the payee releases with `Vesting.vest`, since a lock stays after its schedule has ended [native-modules/vesting].
- **Costs:** `transferKeepAlive` 0.0149 to 0.0150 tPOT, a `batchAll` of two transfers 0.0194 to 0.0196, `Assets.transfer` 0.0146, an Ethereum transfer 0.0124 [amounts-and-fees/native-fees]; plus 0.1 tPOT for each account a payment creates [errors/E17, errors/E54].
- **Not known:** a sent `Utility.batchAll` from a 0x account [open-questions/not-known]; a sent `Vesting.vestedTransfer` (dry runs only [native-call-errors/vesting]); fees paid in another asset [amounts-and-fees/paying-fees-another-asset]; how many transfers fit in one `batchAll` within a block (only the 10922-call limit is in these notes): grow the batch step by step, dry-running each.

## Escrow and conditional payments

- **Choose it when:** money is held until something happens: delivery confirmed, a milestone, a deposit returned, a sealed bid, a savings circle, a refund after a deadline.
- **The chain has:** no escrow module among the 69 [native-modules/69-modules]. Multisig: an account of M of N members that runs a call once enough of them approve it, with no code [native-modules/multisig-rules]; its id comes from the sorted members and the threshold [addresses/module-accounts]. A contract can hold Assets tokens at their ERC-20 addresses [contracts/tokens-assets-module-seen] and reads `block.timestamp` as on Ethereum [contracts/evm]; nothing runs by itself at a time, since the Scheduler refuses a signed account [native-call-errors/scheduler].
- **Decide:** "If the buyer never confirms, who decides: an arbiter, or code after a deadline?" Default: an arbiter, in a 2-of-3 multisig of buyer, seller and arbiter: no code, and 9.27 tPOT reserved from the opener until the call runs [native-modules/multisig-rules]. Code: a contract on the contract route, also for many deals at once or a fee formula, holding 0.078 x L + 13.11 tPOT for its code [amounts-and-fees/deploying-contract] and 3.8415 per new slot a deal writes [amounts-and-fees/contract-storage-slot]; a deadline acts only when someone sends the call.
- **Traps:**
  - The same members and threshold always give the same multisig account [addresses/module-accounts], so deals at once would share one balance: one open deal per account.
  - Open with `asMulti` and the full call, since `approveAsMulti` never runs it; the extrinsic succeeds even when the inner call fails: read `MultisigExecuted` [errors/E16]. A dry run of the approval that runs the call is expected to hide the inner result [native-call-errors/measured].
  - pUSD and other tokens that are not sufficient cannot reach an account with no tPOT: give the escrow account 0.1 tPOT first [native-call-errors/assets].
  - In a contract: check `balanceOf` and `allowance` before each token call and revert with your own reason [errors/E40]; keep each deal at or above `minBalance`, or a payout moves the whole balance, other deals' funds too [errors/E42]; never attach tPOT to a token call [errors/E44]; pay tPOT with `call{value: v}("")`, check the result, guard against re-entry [errors/E48]. Root can replace a contract's code: say so in what the escrow promises [networks/admin-key].
- **Plan (multisig):**
  1. Members: `convert_address` and `get_account` of buyer, seller and arbiter (a MetaMask member counts as its fallback form [errors/E4]); compute the multisig account in a script with the formula in [addresses/module-accounts] (no tool derives it; the script signs nothing, so it may run before the money check); `convert_address` of it shows that it does not exist yet.
  2. A test buyer with no pUSD buys some in the pool first [blueprints/swap-dex]. The buyer pays in one `Utility.batchAll`: `Balances.transferKeepAlive` of 0.1 tPOT to the multisig account, `Assets.transfer` of the amount (at least `minBalance`), a `System.remarkWithEvent` with the deal id. Dry run: `encode_call` with the buyer as `"sender"`. Check: `read_storage` of `Assets.account(<id>, <multisig>)`.
  3. The release, read-only: `encode_call` of `Assets.transfer` to the seller with the multisig account as `"sender"` [errors/E29].
  4. The seller opens: `Multisig.asMulti` with threshold 2, the other two sorted, `null`, that transfer, and `maxWeight` from `transactionPaymentCallApi.queryCallInfo` [recipes/8], rounded. Dry run: `encode_call` with the seller as `"sender"`. Check: `read_storage {"item": "Multisig.multisigs", "keys": ["<multisig 5...>"]}` lists the open call and its timepoint `when`.
  5. The buyer confirms: `asMulti` with that timepoint, the same call and `maxWeight`. Check: `get_transaction` shows `MultisigExecuted` with an Ok result; the seller's `Assets.account` grew; the seller's reserve is back (`get_account`).
  6. A dispute: the arbiter approves the side it agrees with; an opener takes an open call back with `Multisig.cancelAsMulti`, which returns its reserve [native-modules/multisig-rules].
  - The contract route instead: the buyer approves at the token address [recipes-contracts/2g]; dry-run each deal call with `simulate_contract_call`, and the token move alone with the contract as `"from"`, which names the error the contract cannot see [errors/E40].
- **Costs:** the opener's reserve, 9.27 tPOT for 2 of 3 [native-modules/multisig-rules]; fees 0.0328 to open and 0.0336 to run a multisig with a contract call inside (Oct 2026) [amounts-and-fees/native-fees, contracts/proven-not]; for your inner call, `encode_call` of `Multisig.asMulti` with `"sender"` gives the fee; 0.1 tPOT stays on the multisig account. A contract: its deposit from `simulate_contract_deploy`, 3.8415 per new slot per deal, and 0.1 tPOT held from the buyer per approval [errors/E41].
- **Not known:** a sent multisig with an Assets transfer inside (ours ran a contract call) [contracts/proven-not]; `Multisig.MaxWeightTooLow` [native-call-errors/not-measured]; `asMulti` sent from a 0x account [native-call-errors/measured]; whether a new contract's address can receive pUSD before it holds tPOT: the dry run of the first deposit shows it.

## Multisig or treasury

- **Choose it when:** a team treasury, a shared account, payments that need 2 of 3 approvals, a waiting period before a spend, a hot key or a bot with limited rights.
- **The chain has:** Multisig, `Multisig.asMulti` 0x2401 [native-modules/everyday-jobs-calls], at most 100 signatories [native-modules/multisig-rules]; Proxy, with the types Any, NonTransfer, Governance and Staking [native-modules/proxy-rules], and a delay that needs `Proxy.announce`, a wait, then `Proxy.proxyAnnounced` [native-call-errors/proxy]; `Utility.batchAll`. Safe is not deployed [errors/E19]. The chain's Treasury module refuses spends from a signed account [native-call-errors/treasury]: your treasury is your multisig.
- **Decide:** "How many members must approve a payment (2 of 3, or all 3), and do you need a cap on amounts?" Default: 2 of 3, a native multisig (9.27 tPOT held per open payment; all 3: 11.19) [native-modules/multisig-rules], plus a proxy of a narrow type, with a delay, for a key that may do one kind of thing. A proxy's type filters which calls run [native-modules/proxy-rules], so a cap per payment or per day is a contract on the contract route.
- **Traps:**
  - Each open operation reserves 5.43 + 1.92 x threshold from its opener, 9.27 tPOT for 2, until it runs or is cancelled [native-modules/multisig-rules].
  - `otherSignatories` sorted by account id and without the sender; a threshold of 2 or more; an approval needs the opening's timepoint, or it gets `WrongTimepoint` [native-call-errors/multisig].
  - `approveAsMulti` stores only a hash and never runs the call; the extrinsic succeeds even when the inner call fails: read `MultisigExecuted` [errors/E16]. Every approver must build the same call: round the limits, take `maxWeight` from `queryCallInfo` [native-modules/multisig-rules, recipes/8].
  - A proxy whose type does not cover the call: the transaction succeeds, nothing moves, and only `Proxy.ProxyExecuted` holds the error [errors/E38]; a `forceProxyType` the relation lacks fails with `NotProxy` and still pays its fee [native-call-errors/proxy].
  - Dry runs of `Proxy.proxy` and of the approval that runs the call show only the outer result [native-call-errors/measured].
  - Members and delegates are native accounts: a MetaMask user takes part with its fallback form [errors/E4], sending through the pallets address [recipes-contracts/4].
- **Plan:**
  1. The multisig account from the members and the threshold: no tool derives it, so compute it with a short read-only script by the formula in [addresses/module-accounts] (it signs nothing, so it may run before the money check), and check its forms with `convert_address`. Fund it natively with at least 0.1 tPOT plus the tPOT it will pay out [errors/E17]; a token that is not sufficient, such as pUSD, reaches it only after that 0.1 tPOT [native-call-errors/assets]. Check: `get_account`.
  2. Each spend: `encode_call` of the inner call alone with the multisig account as `"sender"`, then of `Multisig.asMulti` with the opener as `"sender"` (`SignatoriesOutOfOrder`, `UnexpectedTimepoint`, `MinimumThreshold`) [native-call-errors/multisig].
  3. Open; a second member approves with the timepoint from `read_storage {"item": "Multisig.multisigs", "keys": ["<multisig 5...>"]}`. Check: `get_transaction` shows `MultisigExecuted` with an Ok result.
  4. A proxy: `Proxy.addProxy(delegate, type, delay)`; test one covered and one uncovered call, and read each `Proxy.ProxyExecuted`; with a delay, the delegate announces, waits, then sends `Proxy.proxyAnnounced`; `Proxy.removeProxy` returns the deposit [native-modules/proxy-rules, native-call-errors/proxy].
- **Costs:** 9.27 tPOT held per open 2-of-3 operation; fees 0.0328 to open and 0.0336 to run a multisig with a contract call inside (Oct 2026) [amounts-and-fees/native-fees, contracts/proven-not], and for your inner call `encode_call` of `Multisig.asMulti` with `"sender"` gives the fee; `Proxy.proxy` fees 0.0161 to 0.0184 [amounts-and-fees/native-fees]; 0.63 plus 1.98 tPOT per proxy, 2.61 for one, returned by `removeProxy` [native-modules/proxy-rules].
- **Not known:** `Multisig.MaxWeightTooLow` [native-call-errors/not-measured]; which calls each proxy type covers beyond the two measured [errors/E38]; `asMulti` sent from a 0x account [native-call-errors/measured]; how an announced call is refused during the delay.

## Staking

- **Choose it when:** staking tPOT, joining a nomination pool, claiming rewards, a staking dashboard or a claim bot.
- **The chain has:** `Staking.bond(value, payee)` 0x0b00, then `Staking.nominate(targets)` 0x0b05; `NominationPools.join(amount, poolId)` 0x3e00 [native-modules/everyday-jobs-calls]; `minJoinBond` 1 tPOT and `minCreateBond` 10 tPOT, and one pool on Nov 15, 2026 [native-call-errors/nominationpools]; sessions of 1 hour, eras of 6 hours, 2 validators per network [native-modules/staking-numbers].
- **Decide:** "Stake directly, or through the pool?" Default: read first, then join the pool with the minimum. Directly when the project picks its own validators.
- **Traps:**
  - Unbonded tPOT stays unavailable for 672 eras, about 168 days [native-modules/staking-numbers].
  - `Staking.bond` above the free balance is accepted and bonds the whole free balance; a payment after it is refused [native-call-errors/accepted-although-looks-like]. `Staking.bondExtra` takes nearly all of it too, while `NominationPools.bondExtra` refuses [native-call-errors/staking, native-call-errors/nominationpools].
  - Rewards are kept for 84 eras, about 21 days: an older era answers `Staking.InvalidEraToReward` [native-call-errors/staking]; Reported: unclaimed rewards are then lost [native-modules/staking-numbers].
  - `Staking.nominate` of an account that is not a validator is accepted: check `Staking.validators` first [native-call-errors/staking].
  - A bond is a hold, counted in reserved [native-modules/staking-numbers]: what stays spendable is free - max(frozen - reserved, 0.1 tPOT) [errors/E47].
  - `NominationPools.claimPayout` accepts an empty claim [native-call-errors/nominationpools]. A staking precompile is Reported missing, not checked [errors/E24].
- **Plan:**
  1. Read-only: `read_storage` of `NominationPools.bondedPools`, `NominationPools.poolMembers(<account>)` and `Session.validators`; `get_account` for holds.
  2. Join: `encode_call` of `NominationPools.join(<amount>, <pool id>)` with `"sender"`. Check: `get_account` shows the hold, and `NominationPools.poolMembers` lists the account.
  3. Rewards: in code, `nominationPoolsApi.pendingRewards(<account>)` before `NominationPools.claimPayout` [native-call-errors/nominationpools]; a direct staker claims with `Staking.payoutStakers` within 84 eras of the current one [native-call-errors/staking].
  4. A dashboard or a claim bot: [blueprints/bot-indexer-keeper], with a new era every 6 hours [native-modules/staking-numbers].
- **Costs:** a fee per call (`encode_call` with `"sender"` gives it); the bond, held while staked and about 168 days after unbonding [native-modules/staking-numbers].
- **Not known:** `claimPayout` with pending rewards, `NominationPools.unbond` and `withdrawUnbonded`, `Staking.chill`, and what a bond from an empty account bonds [native-call-errors/not-measured]; a bond of 0 answers `Staking.InsufficientBond` [native-call-errors/staking], but the smallest direct bond is not in these notes.

## dApp front end

- **Choose it when:** a web page where people connect a wallet and sign: MetaMask, a native wallet, or both; actions whose fee the user does not pay.
- **The chain has:** both public endpoints answer a page on any origin (CORS `*`) and batches of 101 calls; keep each HTTP request under 1 MB [networks/limits-public-endpoints]; chain id 420420777, typed into MetaMask by hand: RPC `https://test-evm-rpc-v3.portaldot.dev`, symbol tPOT, no explorer [networks/metamask-other-ethereum-wallets]; ERC-20 addresses for Assets tokens [contracts/tokens-assets-module-seen]; the pallets address, through which a 0x key runs a native call with value 0 [recipes-contracts/4]; `permit` on Assets tokens: the owner signs, another account sends it and pays the fee [recipes-contracts/2g].
- **Decide:** "Which wallet do your users have?" Default: MetaMask, with contracts and token addresses as on Ethereum and native calls through the pallets address. Add a native wallet when users already hold 5... accounts: a MetaMask key cannot move its balance with a native transaction [addresses/ethereum-key-signing-native], and the Portaldot wallet is Reported not to sign Ethereum transactions [ethereum-differences/tooling].
- **Traps:**
  - One user has two address forms: show both, and pay the right one [errors/E4, recipes/6].
  - Gas looks huge because it includes deposits: show the cost in tPOT [errors/E2]. The gas fields MetaMask sets here are not measured: put the gas limit and the max fee into the request yourself [open-questions/not-known].
  - A waiting transaction is invisible on the Ethereum RPC, and ethers 6 takes its nonce from that count, so two quick sends get the same nonce: ask the node, and pass the nonce [errors/E12].
  - Multicall3, Permit2 and EntryPoint are absent [errors/E19]; `newHeads` subscriptions fail, so use `newBlockHeaders` or poll [errors/E30]; `eth_sendTransaction` fails, since the RPC holds no keys [errors/E37].
  - `eip712Domain()` is not served: build the permit domain by hand [errors/E41]; the explorers cannot look up a 0x hash: link the block by its number [errors/E20]. The mainnet has no public Ethereum RPC [networks/mainnet].
- **Plan:**
  1. Settings from `get_chain_facts`; on load, refuse a wallet whose `eth_chainId` is not 420420777 [networks/check-right-chain]; on the native side, check the genesis hash [recipes/0].
  2. Reads: balances in both forms [recipes/7], compared once with `get_account`; `eth_getBalance` leaves out the 0.1 tPOT every account keeps [amounts-and-fees/eth-getbalance-shows]; token balances with `balanceOf` (`simulate_contract_call` shows the call).
  3. The first signed action, a small transfer: gas limit = the estimate (+ up to 10 percent), max fee = gas price + one eighth, priority fee 0 [amounts-and-fees/rule-ethereum-transaction]. Dry run: `estimate_eth_transaction` from a test user's address, verdict TAKEN. Check: `get_transaction` with the hash.
  4. Waiting: until its block the RPC shows nothing, so show "waiting", allow one open transaction per account, and ask the node before the next [errors/E12]; done when finalized, about 2 blocks later [networks/blocks-finality].
  5. Native calls from MetaMask: the bytes from `encode_call`, dry-run with the 0x sender, sent to the pallets address with value 0 [recipes-contracts/4]; the receipt shows `gasUsed` 0 even when the call failed, so read the result from the node [errors/E46].
  6. A gasless approval: `permit`, with the domain = the token's metadata name, version "1", chain id 420420777, the token address; the owner still needs an `eth_getBalance` of at least 0.1 tPOT (0.2 tPOT free on the native side, when nothing is frozen) for the approval's deposit [errors/E41].
- **Costs:** users pay their own fees: an Ethereum transfer 0.0124 tPOT, an ERC-20 `transfer` 0.0187, a `permit` 0.0348 for its sender with 0.1 tPOT held from the owner [amounts-and-fees/native-fees].
- **Not known:** the gas fields of MetaMask [open-questions/not-known]; signing a native transaction in a browser extension [recipes/1]; native calls other than a remark and a transfer sent through the pallets address [native-modules/native-call-0x-account]; fees in another asset [amounts-and-fees/paying-fees-another-asset]; the MetaTx and SkipFeelessPayment modules, which these notes only list [native-modules/69-modules].

## Bot, indexer or keeper

- **Choose it when:** a program that watches the chain or acts on it: an indexer, an explorer, alerts, a health monitor, a keeper that sends on time, a payout bot, a staking claimer.
- **The chain has:** every contract event in the node's `System.Events` as `revive.ContractEmitted`, while `eth_getLogs` serves only those of Ethereum transactions [recipes/5c, errors/E6]; `eth_getLogs` with no block-range limit, and state back to block 1 on the node on Nov 13 to 15, 2026 [networks/node-rpc-surface]; `newBlockHeaders` subscriptions [networks/ethereum-rpc-over-websocket]; finality about 2 blocks behind the head [networks/blocks-finality]; `get_block` and `get_contract_events` to compare your output with.
- **Decide:** "Does it only read, or does it also send?" Default: only read. A bot that sends needs a funded test key in its own environment and the nonce rules of plan step 4; anything at a set time is a timer of yours, since the Scheduler refuses a signed account [native-call-errors/scheduler].
- **Traps:**
  - `eth_getLogs` misses every event of a native-path call (`revive.call`, a multisig, a batch), with no error [errors/E6]; a `toBlock` beyond the head answers "Failed to filter logs" [errors/E33].
  - Outer success is not the result: a reverted Ethereum transaction shows `ExtrinsicSuccess` [errors/E9]; a failed call through the pallets address shows `gasUsed` 0 and still paid [errors/E46]; proxies and multisigs report inside their events [errors/E38, errors/E16].
  - Token balances come from `balanceOf` or `Assets.Transferred`, never from sums of `Transfer` logs [errors/E42].
  - The testnet stopped for 13 hours and for 5 hours in Oct 2026, with no status page: check that the head moves before you blame the code [errors/E14]; a receipt was once missing for days [errors/E15].
  - The Ethereum RPC kept only about 256 blocks of state in Oct 2026: store what you need as blocks finalize [open-questions/1]; after a runtime upgrade, decode a block with its parent's metadata [networks/runtime-history].
  - The explorer API returned incomplete lists [networks/explorers-consoles]; at most 4 `chainHead_v1_follow` per connection, and an HTML 413 page from the Ethereum RPC for a request of about 2 MB (1 MB passed) [errors/E52].
- **Plan:**
  1. Connect with the chain checks [recipes/0], and with timeouts and retries: reconnect after 2.5 s, time a request out after 60 s, retry 3 times 2 s apart [networks/testnet-halts].
  2. Test your decoder on two known blocks against `get_block` and `get_contract_events`: 389240 (an Ethereum transaction, logs on both sides) and 463584 (a native `revive.call`, on the node only) [recipes/5c].
  3. Follow finalized blocks; index the node's events by block number, since the two sides share numbers, not hashes [recipes/5]; keep the last block done.
  4. If it sends: one transaction at a time per account, the nonce from `system_accountNextIndex`, the hash written down before submitting, an unclear outcome settled with recipe 9 before the next send [recipes/9, errors/E12]; a dry run in the bot's own code before each send: `eth_estimateGas` of the call bytes from the sender's 0x form to the pallets address [errors/E29], or `system_dryRun` of the signed bytes, which the public node answers today and may stop answering [recipes/1b]; for an Ethereum transaction, its `eth_estimateGas` and the balance check [amounts-and-fees/rule-ethereum-transaction] (while you build it, `encode_call` with `"sender"` or `estimate_eth_transaction` gives the same answer); the key read from the environment, never logged.
  5. A payout every day: each run checks the payees again (form, exists), sends one `Utility.batchAll` with the run id in a remark [blueprints/payments-payouts], and does not start while the previous run is unsettled. A staking claimer: [blueprints/staking].
- **Costs:** reading is free; keep the load modest on these shared nodes (about 19 answers a second over one WebSocket, HTTP batches of 50) [networks/node-rpc-surface]; each send pays its fee [amounts-and-fees/native-fees].
- **Not known:** whether a `logs` subscription delivers logs, and how halts are announced [open-questions/not-known]; how far back the Ethereum RPC will serve state [open-questions/1]; whether `eth_getLogs` serves the logs of token addresses [evm-checks/3].

## Data storage and records

- **Choose it when:** proving that a document or file existed, receipts, anchoring the hash of a payout list, data that others fetch from the chain.
- **The chain has:** `TransactionStorage.store` 0x3300 (1 to 8388608 bytes, kept 100800 blocks, about 7 days, unless renewed) and `renew` 0x3301 [native-modules/transactionstorage], the bytes served back by `bitswap_v1_get` with a CID [recipes/10]; `System.remarkWithEvent`, whose event names the sender and the hash of the text [native-modules/call-encoding]; contract storage at 3.8415 tPOT per new 32-byte slot [amounts-and-fees/contract-storage-slot].
- **Decide:** "Only prove it existed, keep the bytes on the chain, or let a contract read it?" Default: the hash in a `System.remarkWithEvent`, the document kept by the developer. The bytes: TransactionStorage, where 1024 bytes cost about 0.1138 tPOT of fees [amounts-and-fees/native-fees], while 32 new contract slots would hold about 122.9 tPOT [amounts-and-fees/contract-storage-slot]. A contract slot only for a value that a contract must read.
- **Traps:**
  - Stored data is public and should be treated as permanent [networks/data-stored-transactionstorage]: never store anything private.
  - A dry run of a valid `store` or `renew` always answers `BadContext`: check the size, 1 to 8388608 bytes, yourself; `store` of 0 bytes answers `BadDataSize` [errors/E53].
  - `renew` needs the block and index of the last `Stored` or `Renewed` event; a renew of nothing is included, fails and pays its fee [errors/E53].
  - `bitswap_v1_get` takes the CID, not the 0x hash [errors/E53]; `TransactionStorageApi_indexed_transactions` takes the block number as 4 little-endian bytes [native-modules/transactionstorage].
  - The Ethereum RPC answered a request of 2,000,128 bytes with an HTML 413 page and took one of 1,000,128 bytes; the Substrate node's limit is not known, so keep each request under 1 MB [errors/E52].
- **Plan:**
  1. Size and privacy first, then the fee: `paymentInfo` in the script [recipes/10].
  2. Anchor a hash: `System.remarkWithEvent`. Dry run: `encode_call` with `"sender"`. Check: `get_transaction` shows `System.Remarked` with the sender and the hash; record the block and index.
  3. Store bytes: `TransactionStorage.store` [recipes/10]. Check: `get_transaction` shows `TransactionStorage.Stored` with `index` and `contentHash`; read the bytes back with recipe 10's `read-stored.mjs`, which checks the content hash.
  4. Keep it: `renew(block, index)` within 100800 blocks, sent by a bot of yours [blueprints/bot-indexer-keeper]; a dry run of `renew` still catches `RenewedNotFound` [errors/E53].
- **Costs:** a 16-byte store paid 0.0126 tPOT plus 1160 base units; 1024 bytes about 0.1138 and 65536 bytes 6.5652 by fee query; a renew 0.0117; a `System.remark` of a few bytes 0.0114 to 0.0126 [amounts-and-fees/native-fees]; 4096 bytes 0.4210 [native-modules/transactionstorage].
- **Not known:** whether data past its retention is still served, and `check_proof` [open-questions/not-known, errors/E53]; the Substrate node's request-size limit, which matters for large stores [open-questions/not-known]; a `store` sent from a 0x account.

## Bridge, oracle or proofs

- **Choose it when:** proving this chain's state or events on another chain, a light client, a lock-and-mint transfer, a price feed into Portaldot, a contract that checks signatures.
- **The chain has:** no bridge module: iBridge, named in the V3.0 feature list, is not in the runtime [native-modules/modules-named-feature-list]. The node serves `state_getReadProof` back to block 1, `state_getChildReadProof` for contract storage, `grandpa_proveFinality`, and GRANDPA and BEEFY justifications [networks/proofs-finality-data]; GRANDPA has 2 authorities of weight 1, so both must vote for a block to become final [networks/blocks-finality]. A contract here has ecrecover, sha256, ripemd160, identity, modexp, bn256, blake2f and P256VERIFY; KZG and BLS12-381 are absent [contracts/evm]. A price from a native pool comes from the quote runtime call [recipes-contracts/2g].
- **Decide:** "Facts out of Portaldot (proofs), or data into it (a feed)?" Default: out of it: the node gives the proofs, and no contract is needed. A feed: a contract on the contract route that keeps the latest values in the same slots, since only a new slot holds 3.8415 tPOT [amounts-and-fees/contract-storage-slot], updated by a bot of yours. Do not rely on the Oracle module: what its single key holds is not known [open-questions/not-known].
- **Traps:**
  - The Ethereum block's `stateRoot` is not a state root: prove against the Substrate header's `stateRoot` [ethereum-differences/transactions-blocks-logs, networks/proofs-finality-data]; link the two sides by block number, never by hash [recipes/5].
  - The public node gives no MMR proofs [errors/E52]; a call to BLS12-381 returns no data and no error [errors/E31].
  - Recent blocks carry no justification: subscribe instead of reading blocks [networks/proofs-finality-data]; the validator set can change at each era [networks/blocks-finality].
  - Never give another chain a native account's 0x form: no key stands behind it [addresses/native-accounts-0x-form].
  - The testnet stopped twice: treat a stop as pending, not as failure [networks/testnet-halts]. Both networks have a Sudo key, and root can replace contract code: state it in what the bridge claims [networks/admin-key].
  - Answers of about 1.8 MB come whole: set long timeouts and large frame limits [networks/limits-public-endpoints].
- **Plan (proofs out):**
  1. Finality: subscribe with `grandpa_subscribeJustifications`, and follow the validator set [networks/proofs-finality-data, networks/blocks-finality].
  2. State: `state_getReadProof` of the item you need, checked against the Substrate header's `stateRoot` [networks/proofs-finality-data].
  3. Contract storage: `state_getChildReadProof` with the child key `:child_storage:default:` + the `trieId` from `revive.accountInfoOf` [networks/proofs-finality-data]; slot K sits under blake2_256(K) [contracts/contract-storage]. `get_account` shows the contract.
  4. Finality proofs: `grandpa_proveFinality` [networks/proofs-finality-data].
  - A feed instead: the contract route; the updater is a bot with its own key, one send at a time [recipes/9]; dry-run each update with `simulate_contract_call` and read the getter after it; readers use `eth_call`, and events of native-path updates are only in `ContractEmitted` [errors/E6].
- **Costs:** reading proofs is free; a finality proof was 2.5 to 2.7 kB 5 blocks back and 752,767 bytes 3000 blocks back [networks/proofs-finality-data]; a feed contract holds its deployment deposit and 3.8415 per new slot [amounts-and-fees/deploying-contract, amounts-and-fees/contract-storage-slot].
- **Not known:** MMR proofs from a node with offchain indexing, the mainnet validator set beyond its 2 validators, and the Oracle module's storage [open-questions/not-known].
